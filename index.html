Reading the Night Sky

A beginner's guide to what you can see without a telescope

Start with your eyes

You don't need equipment to enjoy the sky. Give your eyes twenty minutes in the dark, switch off nearby lights, and the faint stars slowly appear. Patience is the only tool you really need.

The Moon

The brightest object at night. Watch it over a week and you will see its shape change as it moves through its phases.

The Planets

Planets shine steadily while stars twinkle. Venus and Jupiter are often the brightest points of light after sunset.

The Stars

Stars form patterns called constellations. Learn one, such as Orion, and use it to find the others nearby.

The sky is the same for everyone. What changes is how long we take to look at it.

Quick tips

  • Pick a clear night away from bright streetlights.
  • Let your eyes adjust before you start looking.
  • Use a free star-map app to name what you see.
